Micromouse
Autonomous Maze Robot

A compact autonomous robotics platform for exploring the interaction between mechanical packaging, reliable sensing and high-performance closed-loop motion control.

RoleMechanical · Electronics · Firmware
Project typeAutonomous robotics
FocusSensing · control · optimisation

The challenge

Micromouse rewards complete system thinking: a small robot must sense an unknown maze, form a consistent map, plan a path and move precisely enough to make that plan meaningful. Every gram, millimetre and control-loop decision affects its eventual run.

Engineering approach

The platform is being developed as a sequence of testable subsystems rather than as one large integration problem. This makes it possible to validate mechanical traction, encoder feedback, wall sensing and navigation logic independently before optimising the combined system.

  • Compact chassis packaging that protects the sensor geometry and keeps mass low.
  • Closed-loop wheel control using encoder feedback for repeatable straight-line and turning behaviour.
  • Structured navigation software that separates maze discovery, path planning and motion execution.

What this project develops

This is an ongoing engineering platform, designed to turn theory into measurable behaviour. The next steps are calibration work, repeatability testing and progressively faster autonomous runs.
